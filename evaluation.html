model_training Project Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be judged by industry experts and alumni based on the following key metrics, totaling **100 points**. Focus on the innovative and practical application of Geospatial technology with a robust **AI component**.

memory

AI & Geospatial Integration

40%

How effectively is the **AI model** used to process, analyze, or predict outcomes based on the **geospatial data**? Is the integration innovative and central to the solution?

code

Technical Execution

30%

Quality of the code, stability of the application, and **demonstrable functionality** of the prototype. Assessment of chosen technologies and use of version control (Repo URL).

trending_up

Real-World Impact & Viability

20%

Potential to solve the chosen hackathon theme's problem effectively, **scalability**, and addressing a genuine market or community need within DeKUT or Kenya.

present_to_all

Presentation & Clarity

10%

Clarity and professionalism of the video and documentation (PowerPoint/Report) submitted. Ability to succinctly explain the problem, solution, and technology stack.

gavel Jury's Decision

The evaluation process heavily weights **AI integration (40%)**, reflecting the core challenge of the G-DEV Hack 2025. Judges' decisions are final.

Review the official rules document for specific sub-criteria details.
